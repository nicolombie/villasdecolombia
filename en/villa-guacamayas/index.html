The retreat

Absolute peace at 740 metres, perfect weather all year round

Five minutes from the Cauca River and the Puente Iglesias interchange, the villa hides in 4 hectares of tropical gardens where macaws land in the fruit trees every morning. Warm, gentle weather, sunsets over the cordillera and total silence: the perfect escape for families and groups of friends.

The entire house is yours: no one else, just your group, the house managers who look after every detail, and nature all around.

  • 100% private property, exclusively for your group
  • On-site staff: cooking, daily housekeeping and BBQ
  • 1h45 from Medellín via the Pacífico highways

Frequently asked questions

Before you book

What is the minimum stay?

2 nights. On holiday weekends and in high season (December–January, Easter week), bookings usually cover the full holiday weekend.

Is the staff included?

Yes: our house managers live on the property and take care of housekeeping, cooking and the BBQ. For groups of more than 8, an extra helper is available for COP 200,000/day.

How do I book?

Message us on WhatsApp with your dates and group size. We confirm availability the same day and secure your booking with a deposit by bank transfer (Bancolombia/Nequi).

Are pets and children welcome?

Children are very welcome (there are games and play areas for them). Pets are accepted — just let us know when booking.

Is the internet connection good?

Yes: two independent connections, including Starlink. Reliable enough even for remote work.
