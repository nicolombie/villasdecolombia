The house

Medellín at your feet, nature at your back

On the Las Palmas road, just 20 minutes from Provenza and 30 from José María Córdova airport, Villa Esmeralda combines the best of two worlds: crisp mountain air and the entire city glittering at your feet every night.

Designed by architects and finished in noble materials — travertine, marble, fine woods — the house opens onto the valley through floor-to-ceiling windows and borders a nature reserve alive with birds.

  • Outdoor jacuzzi with the best view in Medellín
  • House manager and housekeeper: breakfasts, housekeeping and BBQ
  • Heated floors throughout, app-controlled

Frequently asked questions

Before you book

Who is Villa Esmeralda ideal for?

Families (we provide a crib, high chair, playroom and kids' room), groups of friends and teams on a workation. Parties are not allowed.

Is the staff included?

Yes: Ángel (house manager) and Wendy (housekeeper) take care of housekeeping, breakfasts and the BBQ throughout your stay.

How does check-in work?

Self check-in via smart lock between 3 p.m. and 11 p.m.; check-out at 11 a.m. We send you an arrival guide with photos, and the house manager welcomes you.

Does it get cold in Las Palmas?

Evenings are cool (mountain climate) — that's why the house has heated floors in every room, a fireplace and a hot outdoor jacuzzi.

How do I book?

Message us on WhatsApp with your dates and group size. We confirm availability the same day.
