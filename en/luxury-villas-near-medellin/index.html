Frequently asked questions

Before booking your villa

How much does a luxury finca rental near Medellín cost?

It depends on the season, the number of guests and the number of nights. Booking direct on WhatsApp always gets you the best rate, with no platform fees: message us with your dates and we'll send a quote the same day.

Do the villas include staff?

Yes. At both properties the staff take care of cleaning, cooking and the barbecue during your stay: live-in house managers at Villa Las Guacamayas, and Ángel and Wendy at Villa Esmeralda.

Which one is better for a large group?

Villa Las Guacamayas: it sleeps up to 16 guests in 5 bedrooms, each with a private bathroom, with a 15 m infinity pool and 4 hectares of gardens. Villa Esmeralda is ideal for groups of up to 9 guests.

How far are they from Medellín?

Villa Esmeralda is 20 minutes from Provenza via the Las Palmas road. Villa Las Guacamayas is 1 h 45 via the Pacífico 1 and 2 highways, in Southwest Antioquia, near Jericó.

Are children and pets welcome?

Children are welcome at both villas (crib, high chair and play areas available). Pets are welcome at Villa Las Guacamayas; for Villa Esmeralda, please ask us when booking.
